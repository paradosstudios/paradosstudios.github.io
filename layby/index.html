Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Layby ("the app"), published by ParadosStudios ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your level progress, stars, hints and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views, ad taps and app launches
  • Diagnostics and crash information

Google processes this information under its own policies. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Data security and retention

Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not store any of it on our own servers. Google retains it as described in its privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
